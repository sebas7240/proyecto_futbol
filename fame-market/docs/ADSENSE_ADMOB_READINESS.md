# Fame Plays - AdSense and AdMob Readiness

Fame Plays should be presented as a free entertainment game, not as a financial
product, gambling product, crypto product, or real investment simulator.

## Current Positioning

- FameCoins are fictional points.
- Users do not deposit money.
- Users do not buy FameCoins.
- FameCoins cannot be withdrawn, exchanged, transferred, sold, or converted.
- Public figures are referenced for informational and game-context purposes.
- Rewards, when enabled, are promotional, manual, and subject to anti-fraud
  review.

## Public Language Rules

Use:

- Liga de atencion.
- Jugada.
- Sumar apoyo.
- Retirar apoyo.
- Equipo ficticio.
- Recompensa promocional.

Avoid in public marketing:

- Inversion.
- Bolsa.
- Acciones reales.
- Trading.
- Rentabilidad.
- Deposito.
- Retiro.
- Compra de FameCoins.

Negative disclaimers can still say that Fame Plays is not an investment, not a
bet, and not a crypto asset.

## Rewards

If a season includes USDT rewards:

- The ranking page must show the required user milestone.
- The rules must state that rewards are not automatic.
- The top must pass anti-fraud review.
- The payment is manual.
- The wallet is only a public destination address.
- Fame Plays never asks for private keys or seed phrases.
- The reward does not convert FameCoins into money.

## Chat and User Generated Content

During initial ad reviews:

- Do not place ads inside the chat.
- Do not float ads over voice notes, reports, or moderation controls.
- Keep report, mute, block, reset, rate limit, and cleanup tools enabled.
- Hide or remove reported abusive content quickly.

## YouTube and External Data

- YouTube data is display-only unless a separate derived-metrics approval is
  obtained.
- YouTube content must not be presented as owned by Fame Plays.
- The game should keep YouTube signals separated from independent attention
  signals until approved.
- External data pages should explain source limits and attribution clearly.

## Original Content

Before applying broadly:

- Keep `/guias`, `/reglas`, `/privacidad`, `/metodologia`, and `/derechos`
  accessible without login.
- Publish original guides or recaps that explain how the game works.
- Avoid pages that are only automated data with no explanation.
